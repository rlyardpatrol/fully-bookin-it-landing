Where service businesses actually lose jobs

It’s rarely the work. It’s the three leaks between “someone found you” and “job booked” — and all three happen while you’re busy working.

🌙

After-hours questions

Most people shop for services at night. If nobody answers “how much?” and “do you cover my town?” in the moment, they don’t wait — they move on.

🕳️

Half-finished quotes

A customer starts your quote form, gets distracted, and never finishes. On most sites that lead vanishes — no name, no number, no second chance.

📵

Follow-up that never happens

You meant to call that quote back. Then a job ran long, the phone rang twice, and a competitor who followed up in an hour got the work.
